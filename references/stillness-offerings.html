Sessions & Offerings

Every session is
felt, not just attended.

We don't offer classes. We create experiences — designed around what your body, mind, and nervous system actually need. No experience required. Just willingness to show up.

One modality is powerful.
Two together is transformative.

Each offering below stands on its own — but many of our sessions weave modalities together intentionally. Sound and breathwork. Floating and meditation. Movement and stillness. We'll always let you know when a session combines approaches, so you can choose what feels right for where you are.

01

Sound Healing &
Vocal Yoga

Sound · Voice · Vibration

Lie back and let sound move through you. This session combines therapeutic sound healing — using bowls, chimes, and instruments chosen for their effect on the nervous system — with the power of your own voice. Vocal yoga invites you to hum, tone, and breathe sound outward. No singing ability required. This isn't performance. It's release.

Most people leave quieter than they arrived. Some feel things they've been sitting with for months finally loosen.

02

Breathwork

Breath · Nervous System · Release

Your breath is one of the most direct ways to change how you feel — and most of us have never been taught to use it. In these guided sessions, you'll move through intentional breathing patterns designed to regulate your nervous system, clear what's stuck, and create a felt shift in your body and mind.

Led by our certified breathwork facilitator, these sessions are held with care and without pressure. You already know how to breathe. We'll just help you do it differently.

03

Floating
Sound Sessions

Water · Sound · Stillness

Where Stillness began. Our floating sound sessions take place outdoors on the water — the same experience that started it all in summer 2024. You float while sound moves through the air and the water around you. It's difficult to put into words. The combination of buoyancy, nature, and intentional sound creates something that a studio simply cannot replicate.

Seasonal availability. When these sessions run, they fill quickly.

Sessions that
weave together.

Some of our most powerful offerings combine two modalities intentionally. These aren't layered by accident — each pairing is chosen because the combination creates something neither can achieve alone. We'll always name what's in each session so you know what to expect.

◈
Sound Journey + Breathwork
Breathwork primes your nervous system to receive. Sound then moves through a body that's already open. The result is a depth of release that a single modality rarely reaches on its own.
◈
Floating + Breathwork
Water removes the weight of the body. Breathwork clears the noise of the mind. Together they create conditions for a stillness that's nearly impossible to access in daily life.
◈
Meditation + Sound
Guided meditation leads you in, and sound holds you there. For those who find silence difficult to sit in, this combination offers a gentle way to stay present without fighting your own mind.
