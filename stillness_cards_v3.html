How to use them

Simple enough to
actually do.

One card at breakfast

Before phones, before school talk. Read it together or let your child read it alone.

In the car

Nobody has to make eye contact. Pull a card before you leave. The real answers come easier this way.

After lights are low

When a child is soft and open just before sleep. This is when the questions land deepest.

Answer honestly

Children know when they're being given the safe version. Give them the real one.
