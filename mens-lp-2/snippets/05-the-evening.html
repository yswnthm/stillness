The evening

What actually happens in the room.

No experience needed. No one is asked to share. Wear something comfortable — mats, blankets and journals are provided.

When Last weekend of every month · choose one of 3 groups
Where The Shop Theatre · [street address], Vancouver
Group About 13 men per group · same group all year · 40 seats total
  1. [20 MIN]

    Arrive & ground

    Tea, quiet conversation, phones away. Nothing to perform.

  2. [25 MIN]

    Story & breath

    A short, honest talk on pressure and rest, then guided breathwork to drop out of your head.

  3. 45 MIN

    Live sound journey

    Lie down. Crystal bowls and live instruments do the work. No effort required.

  4. [30 MIN]

    Reflect & close

    Journaling prompts, optional sharing, and a clear head for the drive home.
