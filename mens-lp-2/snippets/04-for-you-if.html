This is for you if

You recognise yourself in at least one of these.

01

You're always "fine"

On the outside you've got it together. But there's a version of you that's exhausted — and he hasn't had space to speak in a while.

02

Rest feels unearned

Slowing down triggers guilt. You rest when you collapse, not because you choose to. You're ready for that to change.

03

You want something real

You've tried the gym, the app, the night out. You want something you feel in your body, not just your head.
