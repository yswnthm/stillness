Questions

Before you walk in.

I've never done anything like this. Will it be weird? +

Most men in Session 01 hadn't either. You mostly lie down and listen. Nothing is asked of you that you don't choose.

Do I have to talk or share? +

No. Sharing is always optional. Plenty of men stay quiet all evening.

What if I miss a month? +

Swap into one of the other two groups that weekend if there's room, or send a friend in your place.

What should I wear or bring? +

Comfortable clothes and warm socks. Mats, blankets, journals and tea are provided.

Is this therapy? +

No. It's a guided group wellness evening, not a replacement for professional mental-health care.

What's your refund policy? +
