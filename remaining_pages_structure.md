# Stillness Co. - Remaining Page Structures (Draft)

Based on recent discovery answers and the established design direction, here are the proposed structures for the remaining pages (Shop, Sacred Offerings, and Blog).

### 1. Shop (E-Commerce)

**Goal:** Clean, beautiful, and premium e-commerce experience to replace the current standard WooCommerce look, driving product sales as a top priority.

- **Hero Title:** "Tools for your stillness practice."
- **Featured Categories (Grid/Tabs):**
  - Ritual Kits
  - Essentials
  - Subscriptions
- **Product Grid:** High-quality imagery, minimalist product cards with rounded corners. Dark text on soft backgrounds.
  - Hover states: Subtle fade or secondary image reveal.
  - Action: "Add to Cart" and price clearly visible.
- **Value Proposition Bar:** "Sustainable packaging | Free shipping over $100"

---

### 2. Product Page (Single Item)

**Goal:** Create a high-converting, immersive, and sensory experience for individual physical goods or subscriptions.

- **Main Product Showcase (Split Layout):**
  - **Left (Sticky Media Gallery):** Large, high-resolution product images. Minimalist carousel or stacked scrolling images.
  - **Right (Product Details):**
    - Breadcrumbs (e.g., Shop / Essentials / Stillness Mat)
    - Product Title & Price (using `Lucy Rose` for title, clean sans-serif for price).
    - Short, evocative description.
    - Options/Variants (Size, Color, One-time vs Subscription).
    - Clear, prominent [Add to Cart] button.
- **Deep Dive / The Details:**
  - Full-width accordion or tabbed section for: Materials & Care, Sustainability, How to Use, Shipping details.
- **Social Proof / Reviews:**
  - Simple, elegant review section (Star ratings, user quotes).
- **Related Offerings:**
  - "Pairs well with..." (Cross-selling 2-3 complimentary shop items).

### 6. Blog (Journal)

**Goal:** Storytelling that sparks a quiet pull, educating users on wellness practices, and aiding organic discovery (SEO).

- **Hero Section:** "Notes on Stillness" or "The Journal".
- **Featured Article:** Large highlight card of the latest or most popular post (e.g., "Why We Built Canada's First Floating Bath").
- **Article Grid:** 2 or 3-column masonry/grid layout for past articles.
  - Elements: Category tag, Date, Title, "Read More" link.
- **Newsletter CTA:** Integrated seamlessly between rows of articles to capture emails.
