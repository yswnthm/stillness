Frequently Asked Questions

01

What is the typical group size for your retreats?

Our immersions are strictly limited to an intimate group size, typically around 12 participants. This preserves the sanctity of the space and ensures everyone receives individualized support.

02

Do I need prior experience with meditation or breathwork?

No prior experience is necessary. Our guided sessions are fully accessible to all levels, from beginners to experienced practitioners. We meet you exactly where you are.

03

Are the accommodations and meals included?

Yes. The booking cost covers luxury boutique lodging, organic vegetarian meals, all daily practices, and integration materials during your stay.

04

Can I attend if I have specific dietary requirements?

Absolutely. Our culinary team accommodates most dietary restrictions, including vegan, gluten-free, and specific allergies, provided we have advance notice.

05

What should I pack for the retreat?

Comfortable, breathable clothing for practices, walking shoes for nature immersions, and a journal. A detailed preparation and packing guide will be sent upon booking.
