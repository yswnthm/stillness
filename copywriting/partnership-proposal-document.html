Pure passive income. You connect us with a client who already trusts you. We close the deal. You get paid without doing extra work.

You want transparency with your clients? No problem.

You hop on a call with the client first. You know them best — they trust you.

You introduce us on the call or via message. We take it from there — discovery, proposal, building, delivery.

Step 4: Build

We handle everything. You don't need to manage anything.

Step 5: Payment & Commission

Client pays. We transfer your commission within 48 hours.

You stay in the loop:
