# About Page Copywriting Variations

This document contains 4 variations for each copywriting section of the About page.
- **Variation 1: Poetic / Narrative**
- **Variation 2: Direct / Clear**
- **Variation 3: Scientific / Technical**
- **Variation 4: Original**

---

## Section: Brand Story
**Component**: `BrandStory.tsx`

### Variation 1: Poetic
- **Heading**: The noise ends here. The return begins within. (9 words)
- **Body**: Stillness is more than a name; it is an invitation. Based in Greater Vancouver, we curate rhythmic sanctuaries that allow the modern soul to exhale, recover, and remember its own resonance. (32 words)
- **Body 2**: We began on the water, under the open sky. We moved indoors to find that the same depth exists in the quiet of an intentional room. (25 words)

### Variation 2: Direct
- **Heading**: Professional Wellness Experiences Focused on True Recovery. (8 words)
- **Body**: Stillness provides high-quality wellness sessions in Greater Vancouver. We specialize in floating sound healing, breathwork, and meditation designed to reduce stress and improve mental clarity. (27 words)
- **Body 2**: Starting with outdoor aquatic sessions, we have expanded to indoor studio practices that provide consistent, intentional environments for personal well-being and recovery. (25 words)

### Variation 3: Scientific
- **Heading**: Physiological Regulation Through Intentional Environmental Design. (8 words)
- **Body**: Stillness is a Vancouver-based facilitator of neuro-somatic recovery. Our protocols utilize auditory stimuli and sensory management to down-regulate the sympathetic nervous system and promote systemic homeostasis. (29 words)
- **Body 2**: Our transition from open-air aquatic modalities to controlled indoor containers allows for precise modulation of the therapeutic environment, optimizing respiratory and cognitive recovery outcomes. (27 words)

### Variation 4: Original
- **Heading**: You don't have to keep carrying it. We started outside. The real work began inside. (16 words)
- **Body**: Stillness is a wellness experience company based in Greater Vancouver. We create real, felt experiences that help people slow down, heal, and come back to themselves. (28 words)
- **Body 2**: In summer 2024, we launched with floating sound healing sessions in the Greater Vancouver area. The water. The open air. The quiet. It was the beginning of something. (28 words)

## Section: Mission Statement
**Component**: `MissionStatement.tsx`

### Variation 1: Poetic
- **Heading**: Peace isn't a place you find. It's a rhythm you become. (11 words)
- **Body**: We believe in the quiet power of starting. Stillness is a sanctuary for the weary and the curious alike—a home for the work of genuine healing. (28 words)

### Variation 2: Direct
- **Heading**: Sustainable Healing Starts With a Single Intentional Moment. (8 words)
- **Body**: Our mission is to provide accessible, high-impact wellness spaces. Whether you are new to meditation or an experienced practitioner, we offer the community support needed to heal. (28 words)

### Variation 3: Scientific
- **Heading**: Facilitating Systemic Recovery Through Consistent Somatic Practice. (8 words)
- **Body**: We aim to provide a robust framework for neural and emotional regulation. Our mission is to democratize access to evidence-based therapeutic environments for organizational and individual health. (28 words)

### Variation 4: Original
- **Heading**: Healing isn't a destination. But you have to start somewhere. (10 words)
- **Body**: Stillness exists because healing is real work — and everyone deserves a place to do it. Whether you're just curious or you've been waiting a long time, you're welcome here. (32 words)

## Section: Our Approach
**Component**: `ApproachSection.tsx`

### Point 01: Experiential
- **Variation 1 (Poetic)**: Feeling over knowing. A shift in the pulse.
- **Variation 2 (Direct)**: Focused on results you can feel immediate change.
- **Variation 3 (Scientific)**: Prioritizing somatic shifts over cognitive processing.
- **Variation 4 (Original)**: Experiential, not educational.

### Point 03: Honest
- **Variation 1 (Poetic)**: No masks. Just the truth of the present.
- **Variation 2 (Direct)**: Realistic wellness without the spiritual jargon.
- **Variation 3 (Scientific)**: Evidence-based protocol devoid of anecdotal mysticism.
- **Variation 4 (Original)**: Honest, not mystical.
