- **Variation 1: Poetic**
- **Variation 2: Direct**
- **Variation 3: Scientific**
- **Variation 4: Original**

## Section: Featured Article Excerpt
**Component**: `constants.ts` / `FeaturedArticle.tsx`

### Variation 1: Poetic
- **Title**: Why We Built Canada's First Floating Bath (7 words)
- **Body**: A decade of noise led us to the quiet. We created a space where the weight of the world dissolves in the water, returning you to your own center. (29 words)

### Variation 2: Direct
- **Title**: The Story Behind Our Signature Floating Bath (7 words)
- **Body**: We designed this center to address the high stress of modern life. Our unique floating baths provide a physical environment for deep relaxation and mental recovery. (27 words)

### Variation 3: Scientific
- **Title**: Designing Canada's Primary Sensory Deprivation Variable (6 words)
- **Body**: This facility was engineered to mitigate external stimuli precisely. Through buoyant suspension and thermal consistency, we facilitate rapid neurological homeostasis for optimal stress recovery. (26 words)

### Variation 4: Original
- **Title**: Why We Built Canada's First Floating Bath (7 words)
- **Body**: The story of how a decade of burnout, restlessness, and an obsessive curiosity about the nervous system led to the most profound and unconventional healing space in the country. (31 words)
