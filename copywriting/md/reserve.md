## Section: Sanctuary Etiquette
**Component**: `template-reserve-etiquette-v1.json` (WordPress — not yet in React app)

### Variation 1: Poetic
- **Heading**: Sanctuary Etiquette
- **Body**: Preparation begins before you enter the waters.
- **Rule 1 - The Arrival**: We kindly ask that you arrive at least 15 minutes prior to your scheduled time. This allows you a moment to decompress in our lounge and transition slowly from the busy world outside into a state of rest.
- **Rule 2 - The Silence**: Our sanctuary is a designated quiet zone designed to support deep inward reflection for all guests. Please maintain silence in all shared areas, including hallways, lounges, and changing rooms.
- **Rule 3 - The Disconnect**: To foster genuine presence and protect the privacy and peace of others, we require that all mobile phones and digital devices be stored securely in your locker upon arrival.

### Variation 2: Direct
- **Heading**: Guest Guidelines
- **Body**: Please review these guidelines before your visit.
- **Rule 1 - Arrive Early**: Please arrive 15 minutes before your session to check in and settle. Late arrivals may shorten your session time.
- **Rule 2 - Keep Quiet**: All areas of our facility are silent zones. Please keep your voice low and silence your phone before entering.
- **Rule 3 - Store Your Phone**: All mobile devices must be stored in your locker. This protects your privacy and the peace of fellow guests.

### Variation 3: Scientific
- **Heading**: Facility Protocol
- **Body**: Adherence to these parameters ensures session efficacy for all participants.
- **Rule 1 - Pre-Session Buffer**: Arrive T-15 to allow physiological transition from ambient stimuli to a low-arousal baseline prior to session commencement.
- **Rule 2 - Acoustic Regulation**: All shared zones are low-decibel environments. Maintain verbal silence to protect the collective parasympathetic state.
- **Rule 3 - Digital Deprivation**: All devices must be secured in designated storage to prevent interference and preserve attentional focus.

### Variation 4: Original
- **Heading**: Sanctuary Etiquette
- **Body**: Preparation begins before you enter the waters.
- **Rule 1 - The Arrival**: We kindly ask that you arrive at least 15 minutes prior to your scheduled time. This allows you a moment to decompress in our lounge and transition slowly from the busy world outside into a state of rest.
