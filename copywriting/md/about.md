# About Page Copywriting Variations

This document contains 4 variations for each copywriting section of the About page.
- **Variation 1: Poetic / Narrative**
- **Variation 2: Direct / Clear**
- **Variation 3: Scientific / Technical**
- **Variation 4: Original**

### Variation 3: Scientific
- **Heading**: Physiological Regulation Through Intentional Environmental Design. (8 words)
- **Body**: Stillness is a Vancouver-based facilitator of neuro-somatic recovery. Our protocols utilize auditory stimuli and sensory management to down-regulate the sympathetic nervous system and promote systemic homeostasis. (29 words)

### Variation 4: Original
- **Heading**: You don't have to keep carrying it. We started outside. The real work began inside. (16 words)
- **Body**: Stillness is a wellness experience company based in Greater Vancouver. We create real, felt experiences that help people slow down, heal, and come back to themselves. (28 words)
- **Body 2**: In summer 2024, we launched with floating sound healing sessions in the Greater Vancouver area. The water. The open air. The quiet. It was the beginning of something. (28 words)

## Section: Our Journey
**Component**: `Timeline.tsx`

### Variation 1: Poetic
- **Heading**: Our Journey
- **Why**: "Most of what we carry started young." In working with adults, a pattern became clear. The pain we process in our later years often takes root before we have the language for it.
- **Decks**: Affirmation Decks. Simple, visual gentleness for children. Designed to be held, seen, and felt.
- **Talks**: Conversation Decks. Cards to bridge the silent gaps between children and the adults who hold space for them.
- **Reason**: Why we built them. Not as a product line, but as an answer to the echoes we heard in our adult sessions. Built for the inner world.

### Variation 2: Direct
- **Heading**: Our History
- **Why**: "Most of what we carry started young." We noticed our adult clients were dealing with pain that began in childhood, lacking the words to express it back then.
- **Decks**: Affirmation Decks. Practical, visual affirmations for children aged 4-12 to use alone or with parents.
- **Talks**: Conversation Decks. Cards that prompt honest discussions between kids and caregivers about feelings and fears.
- **Reason**: Why we built them. We created these tools specifically because of what we saw in our adult sessions—to help kids process emotions early.

### Variation 3: Scientific
- **Heading**: Developmental Protocol
- **Why**: "Most of what we carry started young." Clinical observations of adult clients revealed maladaptive patterns originating in childhood due to a lack of emotional vocabulary.
- **Decks**: Affirmation Decks. Visual cognitive restructuring tools for demographics aged 4-12, facilitating early emotional regulation.
- **Talks**: Conversation Decks. Structured prompts designed to enhance caregiver-child communication and emotional intelligence.
- **Reason**: Why we built them. These tools were engineered as preventative measures against the chronic somatic stress patterns identified in our adult demographic.

### Variation 4: Original
- **Heading**: Our Journey
- **Why**: "Most of what we carry started young." In working with adults, a pattern became clear. Most of the pain people are processing in their 30s and 40s has roots in childhood — in things they were never given language for.
- **Decks**: Affirmation Decks. Simple, visual affirmations designed for children aged 4–12. Beautiful, gentle, and genuinely fun to use — alone or with a parent.
- **Talks**: Conversation Decks. Cards that open real conversations between kids and the adults who love them. About feelings, fears, and what it means to be okay.
- **Reason**: Why we built them. Not as a product line. As a response to what we kept seeing in our adult sessions. Every card is built with intention — and with the child's inner world in mind.
